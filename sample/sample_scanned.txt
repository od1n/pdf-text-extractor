Section 1: Export notes

Sodium-ion batteries are entering commercial production for stationary storage. Their
advantages are abundant raw materials, good low-temperature behaviour and a supply
chain that does not depend on lithium or cobalt.

Certification is the gate to every export market. A battery pack that passes UN 38.3
transport testing still needs product and system certification in the destination
country, and the two must not be confused in a quotation.

Pricing must be built from landed cost: ex-works pack price, dangerous-goods
packaging and freight, insurance, duties and import fees. Anything else is a guess
dressed as a number.
